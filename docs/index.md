# Elgara Shaders

Better materials, lighting, day/night cycles, and anti-aliasing for Dragon Age: Origins.

![Cailan's armor, vanilla and Elgara Shaders]({{ "/assets/img/cailan.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

- **Improved materials**: metal looks like metal, in mods too.
- **Improved lighting**: more natural sunlight and shadows.
- **SMAA**: smoother edges.
- **Exposure**: a brighter or darker picture.
- **Ambient occlusion**: shade in corners and creases.
- **Anisotropic filtering**: sharper ground and walls at a slant.
- **Day / night cycle**: the sun and moon cross the sky (Redcliffe only for now).

See [Getting started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}) and [Options]({{ '/options/' | relative_url }}).
